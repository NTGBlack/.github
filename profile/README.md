### English
We are **NTGBlack**, a small team making free trainers for single-player games.
Each game has its own repository. Releases are tagged `g<game version>-r<revision>` — for example `g1.1.1-r1` = game 1.1.1, revision 1 — so you can always pick the build for your game version.
Download only from this page, our [website](https://ntgblack.web.app) or our [Discord](https://discord.gg/U4ZAwR6mfP), and check the SHA-256 listed in each release.
Help, bug reports and requests → [Discord](https://discord.gg/U4ZAwR6mfP). Like our work? [Support the team](https://ntgblack.web.app/donate).
